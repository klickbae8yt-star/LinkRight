# Preliminary Notes: Email Finder Tools for Job Seekers

> These are the starting notes shared at the beginning of the project, before any verification. Many numbers may be outdated or wrong; the research prompt in `contact-finder-pipeline-research-prompt.md` asks for each one to be re-verified.

## 1. Generous / "Forever Free" Options

| Tool | Free tier allowance | LinkedIn extension | Key features for job seekers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apollo.io | "Unlimited" email searches via web portal (fair use) + 10 export credits/month | Yes | 270M+ B2B contacts; search by job title, company size, location |
| Email permutator + email checker (DIY) | Unlimited, free | N/A | Generate common patterns (first.last@, first@) and check them with free verifiers or the Gmail profile-picture trick |
| Google dorking | Unlimited, free | N/A | Queries like `site:linkedin.com/in "@company.com"` to find publicly indexed contacts |

## 2. Credit-Limited Free Tiers (Freemium)

| Tool | Monthly free credits | Standout feature | Claimed accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prospeo | 100 verified credits | LinkedIn / Sales Navigator email finder, low bounce | ~95%+ |
| ContactOut | ~50 | Work + personal emails on LinkedIn profiles | High for personal emails |
| Hunter.io | 25–50 searches + 50 verifications | Domain search showing the email pattern | ~91–96% |
| Snov.io | 50 | 7-tier verification + built-in sender | Under 2% bounce |
| GetProspect | 50 valid emails + 100 verifications | Bulk-save LinkedIn profiles into folders | ~90%+ |
| Skrapp.io | 50 | Emails from LinkedIn profile and search pages | High |
| Lusha | 5–40 | Direct phone lines, personal emails | High |
| RocketReach | 5 lookups | Hard-to-find executives | ~75–85% |

## 3. Paid / Pay-As-You-Go

| Tool | Entry price | Included | Ideal for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skrapp | ~$29–39/month | 2,000 credits/month, rollover | Lowest cost per LinkedIn email |
| Prospeo | ~$37–49/month | 2,000 verified credits | High-accuracy LinkedIn extraction |
| Apollo.io Basic | ~$49–59/month/seat | 2,500 export credits, sequences | All-in-one find + send |
| Hunter.io Starter | ~$34–49/month | 2,000 searches + verifier | Pattern-based emails, smaller companies |
| Findymail | ~$49–99/month | 1,000–5,000 verified credits | Low-bounce cleaning of Apollo/LinkedIn data |
| Coldcast / Wiza | ~$30–49/month or PAYG | Sales Navigator list export to verified CSV | High-volume outreach |

## Initial Strategy Ideas

1. Stack free extensions (Apollo, ContactOut, Prospeo): Apollo to find names, the others on LinkedIn when Apollo misses.
2. Verify every address before sending (NeverBounce, ZeroBounce, Hunter verifier) and keep bounces under 3%.
3. Limit sending to about 20–30 cold emails per inbox per day.
