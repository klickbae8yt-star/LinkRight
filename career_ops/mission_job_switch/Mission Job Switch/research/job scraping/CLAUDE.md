## Key Rules

- **Tavily**: last resort only, filter job boards, use sparingly (free tier limits)
- **agent-browser**: final spot-check only, NOT bulk (sequential, 10–15s/page). JobSpy replaces bulk browser scraping.
- **curl_cffi** `impersonate="chrome136"`: always use over plain httpx for career portal checks
- Never delete `Job Database.xlsx` without backup
- **JobSpy** (`python-jobspy`): Track A job board scraping — LinkedIn/Indeed/Google/Naukri concurrently
- **sentence-transformers** (`all-MiniLM-L6-v2`): semantic scoring — free, local, no API key
- `scrape_static_jobs.py` and `scrape_browser_jobs.py` are **cancelled** — JobSpy covers their scope better
