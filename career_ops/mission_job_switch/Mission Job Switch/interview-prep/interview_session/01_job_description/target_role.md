## 5. Industry challenges jahan PM ko real impact karna hota hai

Procurement SaaS / e‑procurement space me typical challenges: [procol](https://www.procol.ai)

1. **Change management & adoption**  
   - Users decades se email/excel pe comfortable.  
   - Plant buyers, finance, and suppliers ko naya system adopt karwana tough.  
   - PM ko UX ko itna simple banana hota hai ki “WhatsApp se easy” lage.

2. **Complex integrations**  
   - SAP, Oracle, Tally, custom ERPs, finance systems ke sath bi‑directional integrations.  
   - Data sync issues, master data quality, multiple entities/companies handle karna.  

3. **Category & geography complexity**  
   - Commodities vs packaging vs services – har category ka procurement style alag.  
   - India me compliance, GST, regional vendor maturity, language diversity.  

4. **AI in a high‑risk domain**  
   - AI‑driven recommendations me explainability, auditability important.  
   - Wrong recommendation = wrong vendor / price → business ka direct loss.  

5. **Competitive landscape**  
   - Global tools (Coupa, SAP Ariba, Jaggaer) + Indian players.  
   - PM ko clear positioning carve karna: “fast to deploy, India‑first, AI‑native, easier than legacy ERPs.” [procol](https://www.procol.ai)

Ye sab cheezein tum interview answers me “what are the biggest challenges you see for Procol?” type questions me directly use kar sakte ho.

## 6. Opportunities for future PMs (especially Platform PM)

Platform Product Manager role ke context me tum kya impact create kar sakte ho:

1. **Strong platform & integration layer build karna**  
   - Robust APIs, webhooks, event model jisse ERP, finance, inventory systems easily plug ho sake.  
   - Standardised data models for suppliers, POs, line items, events, approvals – taaki har naya feature aur integration fast ban sake.  

2. **AI capabilities ko productised karna**  
   - AI‑driven commodity classification ko generic, configurable service banana (different industries, taxonomies). [procol](https://www.procol.ai/blog/improving-spend-visibility-with-ai-driven-classification-of-commodities/)
   - Spend intelligence: anomaly detection, auto‑savings suggestion, benchmark pricing, risk flags (e.g. supplier concentration).  

3. **Configurable workflows as a platform**  
   - Har enterprise ka approval flow, delegation of authority, risk rules alag hote hain.  
   - Platform PM yahan low‑code/no‑code workflow builder design kar sakta hai jisse implementation faster ho jaye.  

4. **Supplier‑side experience as a growth lever**  
   - 500K+ suppliers hone ka matlab network effects build ho sakte hain: ek supplier multiple buyers ke sath same platform use kare. [procol](https://www.procol.ai/about/)
   - Supplier app / portal ko ultra‑simple banana → faster adoption → buyers ke liye more value.  

5. **Observability, reliability, and scale**  
   - Procurement events me high stakes, specific windows (e.g. live auctions) – downtime unacceptable.  
   - Platform PM ko SLAs, audit logs, versioning, RBAC, data residency jaise infra‑level cheezein define karni hoti hain.

Agar tum apne background ko pitch karna chahte ho, to “platform thinking + integrations + AI awareness” angle se apne projects ko frame karna strong rahega.
