**Legal entity:** Neopolis Global, Inc. (©2025), doing business as Navii.

**What Navii does:** Navii is an AI-powered career companion agent designed to help professionals find jobs and manage their careers. It positions itself as more personal and attentive than traditional job search methods, aiming to "help guide your career and find the jobs that truly feel like you." Free for job seekers; separate enterprise offering for hiring teams.

**Key features:**
- Smart Job Matching — surfaces roles based on a user's goals, values, and career trajectory.
- Career Clarity — guidance on becoming a stronger candidate for desired roles.
- Salary & Growth Conversations — role-plays negotiation scenarios with users.
- Real Voice Feedback / Interview Simulation — practices interviews in real time, gives feedback.
- Proactive job alerts — claims to track 400,000+ daily new jobs and 8,000,000+ total jobs.
- Always-on support — continuous interview prep, insights, and progress tracking; remembers user history over time.

**Positioning vs. alternatives:** Contrasts itself against agency recruiters and LinkedIn — emphasizes speed, personalization, follow-through, and no cost to the user vs. generic/slow/overwhelming alternatives.

**Mission:** Help people be seen as more than a resume. Tagline: "join a world where you're more than your resume."
