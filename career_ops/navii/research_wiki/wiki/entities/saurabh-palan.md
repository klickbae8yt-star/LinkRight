# Saurabh Palan

## Basic Information
- **Role:** Co-founder and CTO, [[Navii]]
- **Location:** Mountain View, CA, United States
- **Education:** University of Pennsylvania
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/saurabhpalan/

## Description
Saurabh Palan is the co-founder and CTO of [[Navii]], an AI career-agent startup. His public profile describes Navii as "the world's first AI-powered Professional Agent Network." His career history (per LinkedIn location trail, titles not publicly visible) spans the San Francisco Bay Area (Mountain View, San Carlos, Concord), the Greater Philadelphia Area, and earliest roles in the Mumbai Area, India. Recommendation references connect him to Toyota Research Institute and Nascent Objects.

He holds 7 patents spanning autonomous agents, robotics, 3D-printed electronics, and automotive driver-assistance systems (2013–2020), suggesting a background in applied robotics/hardware engineering prior to Navii. He is also credentialed in product management (Product School SPM®, 2017; Maven PM career course, 2023) and received Google g2g Coaching recognition (Dec 2023).

On LinkedIn, he actively promotes Navii's recruiting/hiring services, posting job openings for partner companies and portfolio funds on Navii's behalf (see [[saurabh-palan-linkedin-posts]]).
