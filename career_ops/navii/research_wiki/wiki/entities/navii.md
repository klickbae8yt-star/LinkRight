## Description
Navii is an AI-powered career companion that builds a personal [[AI Career Agent]] for each job seeker after a ~30-minute voice conversation. The agent reflects the user's thinking style, values, and ideal work fit, then searches for and surfaces matching roles. The product is free for job seekers, with a separate enterprise product for hiring teams that generates job descriptions, conducts candidate conversations, and returns descriptive insights instead of numeric rankings/scores.

Core guiding philosophy: "credentials don't predict who will thrive" — conversations do. This underlies the [[Professional Agent Network]] concept the company markets itself around, and its positioning against resumes, agency recruiters, and generic platforms like LinkedIn.
