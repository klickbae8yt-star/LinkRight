# AI Career Agent

## Description
The AI Career Agent is [[Navii]]'s core product mechanic: after a roughly 30-minute voice conversation with a job seeker, the platform builds a persistent AI agent that reflects that person's thinking style, values, and ideal work fit. The agent then proactively searches a claimed pool of 400,000+ new daily job postings (8,000,000+ total) to surface matches, helps with career clarity, role-plays salary/growth conversations, runs interview simulations with real-time voice feedback, and retains memory across sessions ("always-on support").

This is the job-seeker-facing half of the [[Professional Agent Network]]; the employer-facing half consists of agents that write job descriptions and conduct/summarize candidate conversations, returning descriptive insights rather than numeric candidate scores.
