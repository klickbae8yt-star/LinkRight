# Voice-Based Candidate Screening

## Description
On the employer side of the [[Professional Agent Network]], [[Navii]] replaces resume/application screening with voice conversations conducted by AI agents. Instead of numeric candidate rankings or scores, the output is "descriptive insights" about a candidate. The philosophy driving this, stated by Navii, is that "credentials don't predict who will thrive" — the conversation reveals fit better than a resume does. This method is repeatedly demonstrated in Navii's LinkedIn job posts (see [[navii-linkedin-posts]], [[saurabh-palan-linkedin-posts]], [[aakanksha-upadhyay-linkedin-posts]]), which pitch candidates on skipping resumes/applications for a direct AI conversation, and in partner-company roles (Arbor AI, Stratasys, Vinyl Equity, Gallop Intelligence, and various venture-fund GTM roles).
