# Candidate Evaluation Matrix (6 Pillars - Total: 100 Points)

## Pillar 1: Applied AI & Workflow Automation Experience (Weight: 25%)
* **What to look for:** Hands-on implementation of LLM orchestration (LangChain, LlamaIndex, MCP servers), multi-agent systems, vector databases, or embedding pipelines. Explicit mention of automating business/operational workflows using APIs or tools like n8n or Zapier.
* **Scoring Breakdown (out of 25):**
  * **20–25 points:** Has built and deployed production-grade AI agents, LLM integrations, or enterprise-wide internal automation frameworks.
  * **10–19 points:** Good experience deploying standard AI/ML models or API wrappers, but mostly focused on consumer features rather than internal workflow automation.
  * **0–9 points:** Only prompt engineering or basic theoretical knowledge; lacks actual deployment experience.

## Pillar 2: Core Engineering & Tech Stack Alignment (Weight: 20%)
* **What to look for:** Mastery over the exact stack: NodeJS, NextJS, ReactJS, AWS, and GitHub/CI-CD pipelines. Look for keywords indicating they understand API architecture, rate limits, and cloud infrastructure.
* **Scoring Breakdown (out of 20):**
  * **16–20 points:** Full-stack expertise in Javascript/TypeScript ecosystems (Node/Next) with deep AWS and CI/CD automation familiarity.
  * **8–15 points:** Strong backend or full-stack engineer, but operates in a different ecosystem (e.g., Python/Django or Java/Spring) with some cloud experience.
  * **0–7 points:** Strictly frontend or disconnected from modern cloud-native architectures.

## Pillar 3: Cross-Functional Change Management & Seniority (Weight: 15%)
* **What to look for:** 7+ years total tech experience with at least 3+ years leading initiatives. Look for evidence of enforcing an "AI engineering playbook," training other teams, or standardizing development velocity tools.
* **Scoring Breakdown (out of 15):**
  * **12–15 points:** Senior/Staff Engineer or Technical PM who has scaled an engineering function, defined playbooks, and managed cross-department dynamics.
  * **6–11 points:** Strong senior engineer who leads a single technical team but lacks proven cross-functional organizational influence.
  * **0–5 points:** Sole contributor or mid-level developer with no experience leading organizational shift.

## Pillar 4: Domain & Fintech/RegTech Context (Weight: 10%)
* **What to look for:** Experience dealing with complex data structures, strict compliance, security clearing, audit trails, or SEC/fintech regulations.
* **Scoring Breakdown (out of 10):**
  * **8–10 points:** Direct experience in Cap Table platforms, Fintech infrastructure, WealthTech, or high-security RegTech platforms.
  * **4–7 points:** General B2B SaaS background with high compliance requirements (e.g., HealthTech, CyberSecurity).
  * **0–3 points:** Pure B2C, e-commerce, or agencies.

## Pillar 5: Efficiency Mindset & "Build vs. Leverage" Balance (Weight: 10%)
* **What to look for:** Candidates who explicitly optimize for velocity—using tools like Claude Code, Sentry Seer, open-source building blocks, and free-tier/low-code pipelines instead of writing heavy, custom infrastructure from scratch.
* **Scoring Breakdown (out of 10):**
  * **8–10 points:** Profiles highlighting "developer velocity," "reducing cycle times," or "automation of manual processes."
  * **0–7 points:** High-code traditionalists focusing purely on custom model training or brute-force infrastructure.

## Pillar 6: Readiness to Relocate to Mangaluru (Weight: 20%)
* **What to look for:** Location history, educational background, and career stage to estimate the likelihood of relocating to Mangaluru for a 3-days/week in-office role.
* **Scoring Breakdown (out of 20):**
  * **16–20 points (Highly Likely):** Currently lives in or near Mangaluru/Udupi. Alumni of local universities (e.g., NITK Surathkal, Manipal). Or early in career (flexible to move).
  * **8–15 points (Moderately Likely):** Lives in South India (Bengaluru, Kerala, Chennai) making relocation easier. Mid-level career where moving is feasible.
  * **0–7 points (Less Likely):** Lives very far away (e.g., North/Central India) and is very senior (likely has established family/roots), with no visible ties to Karnataka/Mangaluru.
