2. Domain Knowledge Dictionary (Zero-Knowledge Conceptual Glossary)

Concept 1

Accounts Receivable (A/R) & DSO

A/R: Customers ko beche gaye saamaan/services ka phasa hua uncollected cash.
DSO (Days Sales Outstanding): Saamaan bechne ke kitne din baad paisa aata hai. Target: 60 days ➔ < 20 days.

Concept 2

Embedded Working Capital

Simple Meaning: ERP ya invoicing portal ke andar hi direct instant credit advance link kar dena.
OCTA Advantage: Invoice issue karte hi 80% capital upfront le lo, repayment customer collection se link.

Concept 3

Trial Balance & GL Matching

Trial Balance: Company ke sabhi Accounts (Debit = Credit) ka summary check.
Pain Point: Manual mismatch hone par accounting teams days tak individual ledger entries search karti hain.

Concept 4

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source)

Simple Meaning: B2B payment karte waqt buyer Govt rules ke tahat tax kaat kar baki pay karta hai.
Friction: Section mapping (194C, 194J) & filing errors se severe penalties lagti hain.

Concept 5

MIS Reporting (Management Info Systems)

Simple Meaning: CXOs aur board ke liye daily/weekly executive performance dashboards.
Automation Goal: Zero manual Excel aggregation; real-time cashflow & margin tracking.

Concept 6

Year-End Annual Close

Simple Meaning: Financial year khatam hone par final balance sheets, audit packages aur books Freeze karna.
OCTA Value: 25-day painful audit preparation ko 4-day automated workflow mein badalna.

B. Mandatory Credit Underwriting Checks

PAN & CIBIL Verification: Individual promoter score + Commercial LLP history.
Sales vs Inventory Ratio: Sales > Inventory (Benchmark ~1.2x healthy turnover).
Physical KYB Inspection: Geotagged GPS photos & physical site verification.
GST Regularity Check: GSTR-1 & 3B filing consistency + cashflow validation.
Turnover Benchmark: ~1 Lakh loan limit requires min ~5 Lakh monthly turnover.
Approval Escalation: Asst. Manager Recommends ➔ Branch Manager Sanctions.
