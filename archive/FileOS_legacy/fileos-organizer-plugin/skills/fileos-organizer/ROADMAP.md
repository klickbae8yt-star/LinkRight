# FileOS Organizer — Milestone Roadmap

## Milestone Overview

| Milestone | Theme | Status | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M0** | **Baseline Freeze & Reproducibility** | **COMPLETE** | Inventory freeze, 17 synthetic golden fixtures, output contract definitions, zero-cloud boundary. |
| **M1** | **Data Integrity & Scan Identity** | **COMPLETE** | Non-overlapping accounting equations, truthful byte metrics, `scan_id`, `scan_config_fingerprint`, 7 action preconditions, stale decision tracking, 15 integrity checks. |
| **M2** | **Architectural Performance & UX Gaps** | **COMPLETE** | `--dry-run` apply mode, streaming dashboard IO (100K+ files), incremental cache scan, near-duplicate similarity, visual folder tree, background watch daemon, Oracle VM connectors. |
| **M3** | **Selective Cloud AI Boundary** | **DEFERRED** | Optional, explicit opt-in cloud AI adjudication for Pass 1-3 review queue packets. Zero silent uploads. Privacy-preserving excerpts only. |
| **M4** | **Personal SaaS & Ecosystem Readiness** | **OUT OF SCOPE** | Single-user personal release, optional backup sync, package distribution. Accounts/billing out of scope. |
