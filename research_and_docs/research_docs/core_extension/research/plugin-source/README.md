# LinkedIn Content Engine

A config driven system that learns your real writing voice, then plans, drafts, designs, gates, and schedules LinkedIn content that sounds like you, not like a machine. It works for any role, field, or goal, because everything specific to you lives in your own config and voice profile, not in the engine.

## What it does

- Builds your writing voice from a short interview plus an analysis of how you actually write
- Picks the strongest topic for each post from candidate ideas
- Drafts the post in your voice, with hard rules and a quality bar it must clear
- Designs the matching visual asset
- Gates every stage through a discerning reviewer, including a risk and credibility check
- Plans the post time, the first comment, and the first hour of replies
- Learns over time from what actually performed

## Skills

- **setup** — run once. Captures your settings, then builds your voice profile.
- **daily-post** — produces one finished, on voice, gated post with its asset and an engagement plan.
- **topic-hunt** — finds high signal, recent stories in your field to fuel ideas. Configurable to any niche.
- **profile-search** — finds and verifies people who match your targets. Configurable to any goal.
- **optimize** — reviews performance and improves the system. Weekly or monthly.

## How to start

1. Run **setup**. Answer a few questions, then a short voice interview. This creates your config and voice profile in your working folder.
2. Run **topic-hunt**, or bring your own topics.
3. Run **daily-post**. You get a finished post, an asset, and a first hour plan.
4. Once a week, run **optimize**.

## Engine and your instance

The plugin ships the generic engine, the methods, agents, checklists, and templates. Your instance, your config, voice profile, audience, positioning, and history, is created in your own folder when you run setup. Nothing of yours lives inside the shared plugin, so the same engine serves anyone.

## What it needs

Web access for topic and profile search. File access to your working folder for your config, voice, and outputs. Profile search reads public professional profiles.
