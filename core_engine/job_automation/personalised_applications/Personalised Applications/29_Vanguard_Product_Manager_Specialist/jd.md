About Vanguard 
More than 45 years ago, John C. Bogle had a vision to start an investment company that did things differently. A company with no external shareholders. Where all the profits were invested back into the business and used to lower costs. Evidently, it was as bold as it was brilliant. To this day, Vanguard Group still has no external shareholders. That means no share prices to protect, and no profits to generate for outside owners.  

 

Today, Vanguard is one of the world’s largest investment management companies, serving more than 50 million investors worldwide. For more than 25 years Vanguard Australia has been supporting individual investors, financial advisers, and superannuation members to achieve their long-term financial goals.  

Team & Opportunity

Our Growth and Experience team is responsible for shaping how clients experience Vanguard Australia across digital touchpoints. The public website is a critical front door to our brand, products, and services — supporting client education, engagement, and conversion across key audiences.
 

We are seeking a Digital Product Manager – Public Web to own and evolve Vanguard Australia’s public web experience. In this role, you will be accountable for the performance of the website for defined audiences, working in close partnership with technology, marketing, design, UX/CX, analytics, and risk teams to deliver best‑in‑class, compliant digital experiences.
 

You will combine strong product thinking with hands‑on delivery leadership — translating client needs and business priorities into clear roadmaps, high‑quality requirements, and measurable outcomes. This role suits someone who thrives in ambiguity, takes ownership, and can confidently balance experience, commercial impact, technology, and risk.
 

Core Responsibilities
Product Ownership & Strategy

Own the vision, roadmap, and prioritisation for Vanguard Australia’s public website within defined audience or journey scope.
Translate business strategy, client needs, and regulatory requirements into outcome‑driven web initiatives.
Balance short‑term optimisation with long‑term platform and experience evolution.
Act as the single point of accountability for the public web experience within your scope.
Client Experience & UX Leadership

Act as custodian of the client experience for your audience, from first touch through to conversion and downstream engagement.
Apply UX best practices, accessibility standards (e.g. WCAG), and content hierarchy principles to ensure experiences are intuitive, inclusive, and effective.
Advocate for client‑first design decisions while balancing technical, commercial, and regulatory constraints.
Ensure experience consistency across connected channels and touchpoints.
Work closely with Personalisation and Marketing Automation teams, as well as other digital product teams, to deliver a connected and relevant end‑to‑end client experience, with the public website playing a key role across journey touchpoints.
Delivery & Execution

Lead the end‑to‑end delivery lifecycle for web initiatives — partnering closely with IT from discovery to delivery, launch, and post‑launch optimisation.
Write clear, high‑quality briefs, requirements, and acceptance criteria for technology and delivery teams.
Partner closely with engineering, design, content, marketing, legal and vendors where required to deliver outcomes efficiently.
Be accountable for quality of delivery, ensuring solutions meet requirements, standards, and client expectations prior to release.
Technology & Platform Leadership

Lead best‑practice use and evolution of Vanguard’s Adobe digital experience stack, including:
Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) for content and experience delivery
Adobe Target for personalisation and experimentation
Adobe Analytics for performance measurement and insight
Work closely with technology teams to ensure scalable, secure, and sustainable solutions.
Understand technical constraints and opportunities and incorporate these into product decisions and roadmap planning.
Data, Insights & Optimisation

Analyse website performance using quantitative and qualitative insights to identify opportunities to improve engagement, conversion, and task completion.
Define and track success metrics aligned to client outcomes and business objectives.
Translate data into clear, actionable recommendations for stakeholders.
Drive a culture of continuous improvement through optimisation and experimentation (e.g. A/B testing, content and UX iteration).
Recognise data as a strategic asset, ensuring public web experiences are designed to responsibly support client insight and understanding, aligned to privacy, consent, and regulatory expectations.
External Environment & Industry Awareness

Stay across changes in the external environment impacting web experiences, including:
Search, discovery, and GEO/SEO evolution
Cookie deprecation, tracking, and consent changes
Privacy, regulatory, and accessibility expectations
Competitor and best‑in‑class digital experiences
Proactively assess implications and recommend changes to strategy, experience, or platform capability.
Governance, Risk & Compliance

Ensure public web experiences meet legal, compliance, brand, accessibility, and privacy requirements.
Work proactively with risk, legal, and compliance partners to embed requirements early in delivery.
Maintain appropriate documentation, approvals, and auditability where required.
Stakeholder Engagement & Influence

Partner with stakeholders across Marketing, Technology, CX, Data, Risk, and senior leadership.
Present recommendations, progress updates, trade‑offs, and insights with clarity and confidence.
Align diverse perspectives while maintaining a clear product point of view.
Lead through influence rather than authority.
