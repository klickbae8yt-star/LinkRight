About the job
At Google, we put our users first. The world is always changing, so we need Product Managers who are continuously adapting and excited to work on products that affect millions of people every day.

In this role, you will work cross-functionally to guide products from conception to launch by connecting the technical and business worlds. You can break down complex problems into steps that drive product development.

One of the many reasons Google consistently brings innovative, world-changing products to market is because of the collaborative work we do in Product Management. Our team works closely with creative engineers, designers, marketers, etc. to help design and develop technologies that improve access to the world's information. We're responsible for guiding products throughout the execution cycle, focusing specifically on analyzing, positioning, packaging, promoting, and tailoring our solutions to our users.

The Payments Compliance Team is protecting Google's business growth while responding to the key threats to Google's global portfolio of products and features. The Team is responsible for building a platform to control regulatory risks associated with Financial Crimes. We work with the Payments Chief Compliance Officer's team of Financial Crime Investigators to detect and prevent illegal activities happening on Google's platform. We ideate, scope, prioritize and execute a roadmap to see needs for separate Google product teams, as well as strategy for financial crimes core detection and prevention platform.

Responsibilities
Own and drive the product goals for key areas within the Payments ecosystem, acting as a growth enabler for Google's multiple suite of products.
Partner with first-party integrators (e.g., Cloud and Play) to understand their business goals, define solutions, and launch products that accelerate their growth.
Advocate a customer-first approach, design solutions that achieve necessary business and regulatory standards.
Build forward-looking strategy and roadmap that leverages Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning (AI/ML) and an innovation-first tech stack to improve the customer experience, optimize operational efficiency, and reduce overall operational costs.
Develop and manage end-to-end projects, work with cross-functional partners to ensure on-time delivery.
